This is a general walkthrough based on the public integration between World and Phantom. Interfaces change  if a step here doesn't match what you see, trust the app over this page and let us know.

## Steps

1. Open the Phantom app or browser extension with a funded Solana wallet
2. Look for World inside Phantom's discovery or dApp surface
3. Follow the prompt into World's market interface
4. Review a market, choose YES or NO, and confirm the trade in your wallet

## What to double check before confirming any trade

- You're inside Phantom's own interface, not a link that merely looks like it
- The wallet prompt clearly shows what you're signing
- You never enter a seed phrase into any website or app screen  Phantom will never ask for it this way

If anything asks for your seed phrase during this flow, stop. See [Staying safe](/learn/staying-safe).
