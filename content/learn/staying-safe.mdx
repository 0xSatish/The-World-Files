Most of the risk around a fast-growing product like World doesn't come from World itself  it comes from everything that copies its name.

## The rules that don't change

- **Never share your seed phrase.** Not with a website, not with a "support agent," not with anyone. No legitimate product needs it.
- **There is no confirmed official World token.** Treat any token claiming otherwise as unverified.
- **Check the domain.** Trade only at world.xyz or through the official Phantom integration  not a lookalike link from a reply or a DM.
- **Unsolicited DMs offering early access, presales, or "official partnerships" are a red flag**, on this product and on every product.

## What this site is not

This is an unofficial community file. We don't have an official app, a wallet connect flow, or any way to move your funds. If a site claiming to be "The World File" asks for a wallet connection beyond reading public information, it isn't this one.
