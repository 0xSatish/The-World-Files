Resolution is the step most people skip past when learning a prediction market, but it's the part that determines whether the whole system can be trusted.

## The general pattern

1. A market is created with clear resolution criteria stated upfront
2. The event happens in the real world
3. An oracle brings that outcome onchain in a verifiable way
4. The smart contract pays out the winning side automatically

## World's public position

World uses Chainlink as oracle infrastructure, which fits the general pattern above  an external, verifiable data feed rather than a single internal decision-maker. Beyond that, World hasn't published deep technical documentation on dispute processes or edge cases, at least not that's part of the public record we're tracking.
