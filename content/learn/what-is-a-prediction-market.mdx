A prediction market lets people trade on the outcome of a real event instead of trading a company's stock or a currency. "Will this happen  yes or no?" becomes something you can buy and sell a position on, and the price moves as new information arrives.

## The core mechanic

Each question resolves to one of two outcomes: YES or NO. Contracts tied to each side trade at a price that reflects the market's collective view of the probability  a YES contract trading near the top of its range implies the market thinks that outcome is likely.

## Why this is useful

- Prices aggregate information faster than most single sources
- Markets exist on things far beyond finance  sports, politics, culture, product launches
- You can express a view on almost anything with a clear yes/no resolution
