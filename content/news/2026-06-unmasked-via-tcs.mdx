Austin Federa posted that the real alpha was in Phantom's disclosures and T&Cs. That single post pulled the teaser account into the open. The mystery globe was no longer mysterious: it was the infrastructure provider behind prediction markets inside a wallet used by millions.

World Cup knockout markets were already live in Phantom at the time. Supported assets cited in the disclosure included SOL, USDC, and CASH. The product was running. The brand just hadn't introduced itself yet.

Around the same window, Foresight News flagged that the world.xyz domain had a reported $80,000 sale or transfer record. That is domain history, not a funding round. There is no confirmed fundraising announcement, and this site does not treat the domain price as one.

CoinDesk followed up the next day with the headline "Mysterious Solana project World unveiled as fully onchain prediction market." HTX asked if the mystery was finally solved. The real answer was quieter than anyone expected: no launch party, no countdown, just a compliance filing that named names.
