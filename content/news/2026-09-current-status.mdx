As of early September, World's public cadence has shifted from product announcements to community-building mode. The account posts follow threads, "the next big thing" energy, and references to "world street bets." The tone is familiar if you have followed crypto brand accounts: less product substance, more community signal.

A KuCoin community note from 6 September asked whether a marketing-heavy Solana prediction market can take meaningful share from Polymarket and Kalshi. That is opinion and market commentary, not a product update. This file records the question because it reflects the current discourse, but it does not endorse or evaluate the conclusion.

## The token question, still open

Phantom's token page continues to warn that the Pump.fun WORLD token is not confirmed as the official platform token. World's own launch materials described a prediction market protocol, not a token launch. Nothing in the official posts since July has changed that.

If you see a token page claiming WORLD is official, check whether the source is @world_xyz or world.xyz directly. As of this writing, neither has confirmed an official token.

## Where things stand

World has a live product inside Phantom. It has Chainlink oracle infrastructure. It has sports markets expanding from World Cup to UFC to full football coverage. It has an architecture described by independent research. And it has a brand presence that generates millions of impressions per post.

What it does not have, publicly: a team page, a token, a published roadmap, or a clear answer on multi-chain deployment. The Robinhood Chain and Hyperliquid posts remain in the X-moment file. The product, as confirmed by independent research, is still running on Solana.

This file will be updated as new confirmed product developments are reported.
