World is live at world.xyz and also reachable from inside Phantom, one of Solana's most-used wallets. That's a distribution choice, not a custody change  the self-custodial model doesn't move when the entry point does.

## What the integration means

- You can get to World's markets without leaving Phantom
- Your wallet still signs every trade directly
- World does not take custody of funds or positions at any point in the flow

## What it doesn't mean

It doesn't mean Phantom operates World, and it doesn't mean World is a Phantom product. They're associated through this integration and through settlement infrastructure, not through ownership  nothing public confirms otherwise.

If you're trading from Phantom for the first time, see [How to open World inside Phantom](/learn/open-world-in-phantom).
