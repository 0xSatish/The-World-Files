This is a culture/X-moment file, not confirmed product news.

On 8 July, @world_xyz posted that after 24 hours of deliberation, World would migrate off Solana onto Robinhood Chain. The account thanked the Solana Foundation and later added that Chainlink would still support World during the migration. CoinMarketCap Academy wrote it up as a migration story the same day.

The post went extremely viral. It was real, not hacked or fake. But what followed did not confirm a completed migration.

## Context that pushed back

Lily Liu of the Solana Foundation replied that the world still revolves around SOL. Raydium quote-tweeted the post saying they would not be leaving Solana. Neither comment was hostile, but both signaled that the Solana ecosystem was not treating the post as settled product news.

## What happened after

The @world_xyz account bio stayed "the solana prediction market." Later official posts continued to reference Solana, Phantom, football, UFC, and Hyperliquid support. On 22 July  two weeks after the migration post  Chainstack's independent technical research still described World as a Solana system, with programs, tokens, and settlement all on the Solana chain.

This site keeps the Robinhood Chain post in the culture/X-moment file. If a completed migration is independently confirmed in later product reporting, this post will be updated and recategorized. Until then, it stays where it is: a real post, a real moment, and an unconfirmed product change.
