As World's name recognition grew, so did the number of tokens calling themselves "WORLD" across various chains. None of them are confirmed as official. World's public materials describe a prediction market protocol  not a token launch.

## What to actually check

- Does the claim come from @world_xyz or world.xyz directly, not a reply guy or a "team member" account?
- Is anyone asking you to buy a token to "get in early" on World? That's not how the product works.
- Are you being asked to connect a wallet somewhere that isn't world.xyz or the official Phantom integration?

If any of those feel off, they probably are. See [Staying safe](/learn/staying-safe) for the fuller checklist.
