This is the main news item of the quarter.

On 1 July 2026, @world_xyz posted what two years of cryptic globe marketing had been building toward. World is live. The Solana prediction market. Available at world.xyz and inside Phantom on iOS, Android, and desktop. Chainlink named as the primary oracle infrastructure. Opening markets: short-duration Bitcoin up/down and the 2026 FIFA Men's World Cup. Settlement in CASH.

The official framing, posted by @world_xyz:

> world is the solana prediction market
> world is live in phantom with chainlink as oracle infra
> world is how the world trades what happens next
> world is just getting started

Phantom quote-posted the reveal. Chainlink posted that World was live for crypto and World Cup outcomes, with expansions to macro, elections, and additional sports in the pipeline.

## How it works, as reported at launch

The product is non-custodial. Funds move only when a user enters a market. Positions, settlement, and redemption happen onchain. Winning positions redeem automatically in CASH  no manual claim queue, no waiting period. Chainlink Data Streams and the Chainlink Runtime Environment were described as the data and resolution stack. Phantom was called the first of several frontend distribution partnerships planned for July.

## Quoted voices

Pedro Miranda, Solana Foundation Head of Consumer, said prediction markets are one of the strongest applications for a high-performance chain, and that World is meant to demonstrate real-time markets, onchain settlement, and wallet-native UX.

Will McComb, Phantom Product Manager for Money Movement, stated that prediction markets need near-instant, low-cost settlement, and that CASH is what enables that for World.

Johann Eid, Chainlink Labs CBO, was cited around the oracle partnership and the broader industry shift away from slow, human-dependent resolution processes.

## Press coverage

CoinDesk, The Defiant, BeInCrypto, SolanaFloor, Crypto Briefing, CoinLaw, Genfinity, and others all ran versions of the launch story on 1 July. The coverage was unanimous on the core facts: Solana-native, fully onchain, inside Phantom, Chainlink oracles, CASH settlement. More sports, geopolitics, and macro markets were promised in the following weeks.

No confirmed official token launched alongside the product. No team was publicly identified. The launch was a product, not a fundraise.
