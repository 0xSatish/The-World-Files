A prediction market is only as good as its resolution  the moment a YES/NO question gets settled based on what actually happened. World uses Chainlink as oracle infrastructure for this.

## What an oracle does, in one line

An oracle brings real-world information onto the blockchain in a way smart contracts can act on. Without one, a smart contract has no way to know who won a game or what a headline said.

## Why this matters for World specifically

- Resolution isn't decided by a single company employee clicking a button
- The data path is verifiable, which supports World's broader "transparent, onchain" claim
- It's infrastructure, not a guarantee  oracle design still matters, and World hasn't published deep technical detail on this publicly
