Part of writing an honest unofficial file is being clear about the edges of what's known.

## Still not public

- Who specifically makes up the team behind World
- Any confirmed official token
- A detailed technical roadmap beyond what's shipped

## Why we're not filling these in

An unofficial site that invents founders or a token to look more complete is worse than one that leaves a gap. The gap is real information  it tells you what to be skeptical of when someone else claims to fill it in first. See [The copycat WORLD tokens problem](/news/2026-08-copycat-tokens) for exactly that pattern in practice.
