This page tracks only what's publicly confirmable. No team names, no funding claims, no invented dates.

## The teaser period

@world_xyz existed for a long stretch as a globe avatar and the slogan "Trade Everything," with no product attached. The account behaved like a brand voice  cryptic, community-heavy, quick to follow back  rather than a typical startup account.

## Identity becomes clearer

Disclosures connected to Phantom, and World's association with Phantom's CASH stablecoin for settlement, began to clarify what the teaser account was building toward.

## Public launch

World launched publicly as a Solana-native, self-custodial prediction market in July 2026, live at world.xyz and reachable inside Phantom.
