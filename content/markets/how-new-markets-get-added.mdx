World moves fast and the community around it moves faster, which means rumors about new markets travel well before confirmation does.

## What we treat as confirmed

- Anything posted directly by @world_xyz
- Anything visible live on world.xyz or inside the Phantom integration

## What we don't treat as confirmed

- Screenshots without a source
- Third-party accounts claiming insider knowledge
- Anything that requires you to act  connect a wallet, buy a token  before it's verifiable

When a new category or major market type does go live, we'll log it here with a date and a link back to the source.
