PolyMart, a community aggregator site, published a product overview of World updated on 4 August 2026. The page described the product as binary YES/NO tokens traded inside Phantom's Predict tab, with automatic CASH redemption when a market resolves.

Two notable additions beyond the launch-day product scope: Formula 1 markets were listed alongside the original World Cup and crypto categories, and the page warned that some regions  including the US, UK, Canada, and others  may be excluded from access.

That geo-exclusion list is third-party reporting, not an official World statement. World's own materials have not published a definitive list of restricted regions. If you want to confirm whether you can access World markets, check inside Phantom directly or visit world.xyz. Do not rely on community product pages for regulatory guidance.

The F1 expansion, if accurate, would represent World's first move beyond the World Cup and crypto markets that launched on 1 July. Sports expansion had been promised in the launch coverage, and F1 fits the pattern of near-term categories that World's account had hinted at.
